HorizonCache-PC (E57)

Cached-endpoint predictor-corrector jumps · FLUX 512px/28 · smoke N=8 · commit 43e46c7 · cluster H100

1 · Executive summary

Verdict: KILL for the cached-endpoint predictor-corrector.

Headline. PC (α=0.5) does not beat plain HorizonCache in any band (best PC−plain = -0.00 dB @ 2.48× on adaptive_1.5), and its endpoint call slightly lowers achieved speedup. Verdict: KILL.

Did PC extend the safe band? No — at 2.8–3.4× PC tracks plain almost exactly (both ≈21 dB); the overshoot is unchanged.

Beat plain HorizonCache? No — PC−plain is ≤0 in every band (small, CI excludes 0 negative at most points).

Beat SeaCache? Only inasmuch as plain adaptive already does; PC adds nothing.

What failed. The cached endpoint velocity is too stale — v_pred≈v_i (curvature ~1–3%), so the trapezoid correction is a near-no-op. The fresh-endpoint oracle ablation does improve quality (+0.29 dB vs plain at the tested τ) but at a full-forward cost that erases the speedup — confirming staleness, not the correction idea, is the problem.

2 · Motivation

  • E56: adaptive headroom stride is STRONG KEEP in ~1.7–2.7×, but overshoots at ~3.4× (all caps) — a measured significant loss.
  • Interpretation: HorizonCache converts SeaCache headroom into small safe σ-stride extensions, but loses when long-stride Euler truncation error dominates.
  • Hypothesis under test: a cheap cached endpoint correction (Heun-like) reduces that truncation error without a full recompute.

3 · Method

x_pred = x_i + Δσ·v_i; v_pred = cached_velocity(x_pred, σ_target; r_anchor) (one extra cached forward ≈1/L, reusing the SAME block residual); x_corr = x_i + Δσ·[(1−α)·v_i + α·v_pred]. α∈{0.25,0.5,0.75,1.0}. Curvature ‖v_pred−v_i‖₁/‖v_i‖₁ with optional cancel/shrink gates. Same regrid scheduler + matched-achieved-speedup protocol as E56; the endpoint call is accounted separately so PC never claims free speedup. Oracle ablation: v_pred is a FRESH full forward (accounted as a full forward).

A fresh endpoint (real forward) changes the outcome where the cached one cannot — but its cost is a full forward per jump, collapsing the speedup. This proves the failure is staleness of the cached endpoint, not the correction idea.

5 · Mechanism

The correction PC needs lives in the block-stack-driven bending of v; the cache freezes exactly that, so v_pred≈v_i and the trapezoid step barely moves.

6 · Failures

  • Stale endpoint velocity (primary). v_pred reuses the frozen residual → curvature ~1–3% (p50=0.014, p95=0.026); the correction is a no-op.
  • Cost slightly erases speedup. The endpoint cached forward adds ~n_jumps/L; PC sits at marginally lower achieved speedup than plain.
  • Curvature gate can't rescue it. The signal is real but tiny and, since PC itself doesn't help, cancel/shrink only removes near-no-op corrections.
  • Not tested at scale: the effect is consistent and significant across 6 operating points at N=8, so a KILL is warranted without a 100-sample run.
